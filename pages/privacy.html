This notice explains what information Kapeng Barako may use, why it is used, and how browser storage works in the current GitHub Pages build.

Last updated: September 28, 2026
01

What information may be collected?

When you place an order, we may ask for your name, phone number, email, delivery address, payment method, order details, and any payment reference you voluntarily enter at checkout.

If you use a customer account, account information, profile details, saved addresses, and wishlist data may also be processed according to the enabled account features.

02

Why is the information used?

Information is used to process orders, calculate shipping, confirm payment and fulfillment details, provide order tracking, and manage customer accounts when that feature is used.

Information should not be used for a different purpose that has not been properly disclosed to the customer.

03

Cookies and browser storage

In the current static GitHub Pages build, no intentional advertising or behavioral-tracking cookie system is enabled. The site uses localStorage for the cart, saved preferences, certain order records, promotions, shipping settings, and other storefront functions.

This browser storage remains on your device until it is cleared, overwritten, or deleted by your browser or by a site feature that uses it.

04

Third-party services

The site may use third-party services for specific functions, such as GitHub Pages hosting, Google Fonts, and optional Firebase customer-account features. When an external provider is used, technical or account information may be processed according to that provider's policies.

The current static checkout does not mean that a secure server-side payment processor is connected. Payment references and order records may remain browser-local unless a backend is added.

05

Security and retention

Information should be limited to what is needed for the stated purpose. Local browser data remains on the customer's device, while data sent to an enabled third-party service is also subject to that service's security and retention practices.

The exact retention period should be set by the business owner based on actual operations, applicable requirements, and any active backend or processor.

06

Your privacy rights

Data subjects have privacy rights under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, including rights to be informed about processing, access, rectification, objection, erasure or blocking where applicable, data portability, and complaint mechanisms provided by law.

For a request concerning your own data, use the contact details provided by Kapeng Barako in Store Settings or on the official contact page.

07

Changes to this Privacy Notice

This notice may be updated when there is a new feature, new data-processing activity, new third-party service, or other material change to the site. The new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
