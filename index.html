How long does shipping take?

Delivery time depends on your location and courier. Your available delivery option and estimated timing are shown during checkout.

What is the difference between Strong, Classic, and Reserve?

Strong is built for a fuller, bolder cup. Classic is designed for a balanced everyday profile. Reserve is positioned as a more nuanced, special-release style.

Can I order whole beans?

Yes. Choose Whole in the grind selector on a product that supports it, then add it to your cart.
